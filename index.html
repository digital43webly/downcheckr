Is a Website Down? How to Check in Seconds

When a website will not load, first check whether it is down for everyone or just you.

Use the Down Checker on DownCheckr.com — type in the address and it tells you in seconds whether the site is down for everyone or just for you.

If it is down for everyone, wait and try again. If it is only down for you, check your connection, clear your browser cache, or try another device.
